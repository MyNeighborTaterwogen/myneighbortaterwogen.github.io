Chapter I

About the Town

If you found it, you were meant to.

Somewhere

past the last streetlight, where the road forgets its name, there's a town that shifts and creaks and leans a little too far to one side. The windows glow. The gargoyles watch. Something in the cellar hums a lullaby. It looks like the kind of place you're supposed to be afraid of.

You don't have to be.

Trempyon is full of vampires and witches, goblins and ghouls — and humans, and everyone else who was told they didn't fit. They all found their way here the same way you did: by being lost at exactly the right moment.

The town won't ask you to change. It won't make you talk about it. It'll just set out a warm blanket, pour you something hot, and leave the porch light on.

It's a cozy blanket and a warm beverage,
not a therapy visit.

  1. I

    Nothing is forced

    The town helps however it can. It never insists. You can just be here.

  2. II

    Leave whenever you like

    The gate is never locked. Stay a night or a hundred years — the choice is always yours.

  3. III

    No time will have passed

    Go home exactly as you left it. No worries waiting. Only the years of experience you chose to keep.

  4. IV

    It's always there

    Finding it again can be tricky. But when you truly need it, the road bends the right way.
