Nobody in Trempyon remembers who lit the first lantern. They only remember that it was already burning when they arrived.

It hangs on a crooked post at the edge of town, right where the road forgets its name. The glass is cracked. The flame is small. It has never once gone out.

## The Lamplighter

Every evening, an old woman in a long grey coat walks the lanes with a ladder over one shoulder. She tops up the oil, trims the wicks, and hums something nobody quite knows the words to.

> "Somebody's always on their way," she says, if you ask. "Best they can see where they're going."

She never asks who. She never asks why. She just makes sure the light is on.

* * *

If you came in on the old road tonight, you passed that lantern. You might not have noticed it.

*It noticed you.*
