"Greetings, Young Charlie. I have a question for you!" Taterwogen beams with his usual vigor.

"Yeah? What is it? I was sleeping." Charlie responds.

"I heard from a concerned mother that her son is missing." Taterwogen leans on his cane.

"I don't know what you are talking about. I'm going back to bed." Charlie pushes the door closed and sighs. He turns around and sees two bright dots punching through the darkness.

"I wasn't done talking, Charles." The distinct English voice loses its usual warmth as the looming figure peers from down the hallway.
