You wake up with a sigh. You slept in again. The night sky is painted with stars, and the moonbeams cast the window's shadow across your floor.

Lately, motivation has been hard to find. But suddenly, an urge hits you. Energy. You decide to go on a walk.

You toss on your jacket, slide into your shoes, and step outside.

You've gone on night walks before; you've always used them to clear your mind on difficult days. But as you walk down the dark street, your mind wanders, eventually lingering on your qualms.

As you walk, you realize: there are too many streetlamps. Down this road, there should only be five before the cracked pavement turns. But you just passed a sixth and are standing under a seventh; the impossible streetlamp buzzes to life with crackling electricity.

"Where... Where am I?" You whisper to yourself, your voice being swallowed by the night.

Your eyes strain against the darkness; beyond the buzzing lamp, you stare slack-jawed at the sprawling, tangled vines. It's a pumpkin patch. The gnarled vines swirl against the massive orange gourds; the sight is beautiful, but how is that possible... It's March.

Curiosity takes you by the hand and leads you farther, past the pumpkin patch, past thick stalks of corn and onto a new street; one you've never seen before. You see kids running around in costumes, knocking on doors.

"Beautiful, isn't it?" A warm, distinctly English voice snaps you out of your awe.

You swivel around to see a tall figure towering above you, though he isn't menacing.

"Welcome to Trempyon." He leans on his cane. "Since you're here, it means you need to be, so... Stay as long as you'd like; you have all the time in the world, Young One."
