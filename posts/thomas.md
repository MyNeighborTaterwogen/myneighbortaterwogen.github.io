"THOMAS!?" The shout echoes down the staircase, followed by a door slam.

Thomas scrambled to his feet, tucked a comic book under the stairs, and ran up the steps to see what his father wanted.

"I went into town today, and that woman in the library asked how you were enjoying your comic book." The boy's father growled, holding a brown bottle in his hand, spittle flying from his lips as the smell of alcohol wafted around him. "I thought I told you that stuff will rot your brain, you ungrateful brat!?"

"I... I just wanted to read," Thomas sputters, trying to get words out, as his face begins to burn and tears form in the corners of his eyes.

"What was that, boy!?" The man shouts, not giving Thomas a chance to respond, swinging his bottle into the side of his head with a resounding crash. "I'm working fifty-hour weeks down in that fucking mine, just to pay the bills around here, just for some spoiled brat that ain't mine to disrespect me."

Thomas's head spun, drenched in cheap liquor and glass; as he tumbled to the floor, the lights dimming, he couldn't tell if the sound came from the bottle or his skull. However, the answer was likely both.

* * *

Thomas came to and looked up at the sky. He tried to raise his hand to shield his eyes from the sun, but couldn't. He was bound.

"I've had enough of you disrespecting me, you ungrateful piece of shit..." The man sputtered between drinks from a clear liquor bottle, splashing Thomas with the foul-smelling liquid, then poured the rest of the bottle on him.

Thomas struggles against his restraints. "People like you belong in hell..."

"Funny ye mention that, boy, because that's where I plan on sending ye." Just then, the man strikes a match and tosses it into the hole with the alcohol soaked Thomas.

Thomas writhes and screams as his clothes light on fire, melting to his flesh, causing immediate second-degree burns. His screams echo through the surrounding forest. As Thomas' light began to fade, he felt shovelfuls of dirt get tossed over him.


Hours later, a deformed hand emerged from the dirt. A hand that was no longer human. Roots and vines wove with the flesh, repairing what he lost in the fire. 

Thomas, now crawling out of what was once his grave, needed a new form; He needed a new body.

He slunk toward the scarecrow, tore it from its post, and crawled inside it. Thomas then grabbed the splitting axe and slunk up the hill toward the house.

Thomas knocked on the door. He knocked hard and fast, the pounding echoing through the house. The old wood cracked under each knock. "Oh, Father..." 

Tim stands frozen with fear as the door flies open, nearly flying off its hinges. "Who the hell... What the hell are you..."

"Your actions have consequences, Father." The last word is laced with venom, the same venom that he experienced daily. "You reap what you sow."

Thomas, without hesitation, swings the axe into the head of the monster that used to be his stepfather, ending his pain, albeit too late. As Thomas removes the axe from the skull with a wet pop and a dull crunch, he hears a scream behind him; a familiar scream: His mother.

Thomas dropped the axe, leaving a puddle of blood and viscera on the floor, before running over to his mother, trying to embrace her in a hug. "Mom, it's me, Thomas!"

The woman screams once more as the scarecrow approaches her, slapping at him and struggling to escape. "I don't care who you are; you're a monster."

Thomas stops, his crooked jaw slack, looking like a scolded puppy. "I ended our suffering... all the years of abuse from that man you decided to replace Dad with after he passed. You're free; we're free."

"I don't need this right now," Joan mutters. "I need you to leave. NOW!"

Thomas pushes past his mother and dashes into the night, eyes filled with unshed tears.
