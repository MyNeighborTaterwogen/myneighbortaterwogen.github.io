"You're one of those Aemis freaks, ain't ya?" A dwarf calls out, having had a dozen beers too many. 

"Sir, please, I am just trying to eat a meal. I've had a long day-" Brent gets interrupted.

The dwarf shoves Brent, hard enough to make the table shift slightly; this shove spills Brent's drink... straight into his food.

Brent stands up and dusts himself off. "What do you want, sir?"

"I know I could take you in a fight, Lad. Right here, right now. Winner gets a free drink."

"I was just leaving; I don't need a drink." Brent grabs his belongings and starts for the door.

"Heard ya precious monastery burnt down ages ago, hoped ya all died." The dwarf belts out a laugh, and the Tavern goes quiet.

Brent stops in his tracks; his eye twitches as memories flash behind his eyes: screams, corpses, cinders.. "Many of my friends died that day; we never hurt anyone. We minded our own business."

The dwarf staggers over and shoves Brent again. "You hoarded all them books, Lad. Though a big brute like you probably never touched a single book."

Brent clenches his jaw; his teeth grind together, the grinding audible. "You..." Brent swivels around, his armor clattering slightly. "You know nothing about me, Sir."

"I know enough to say that your precious god's teachings are a lie." The Dwarf jabs.

Before Brent realizes what he's doing, his hand swallows the dwarf's head, like a child holding a baseball. The dwarf squirms as the firbolg's hand tightens around his head.

With a sickening crunch and a pop, the dwarf falls to the ground, a pool of blood forming below him.

Brent looks down at his blood-soaked hands in horror and runs off into the night.
