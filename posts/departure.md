You wake up in your cozy home; the Autumn air is crisp and fresh, and the scent of fresh apple cider and apple pie dances in your nose.

A familiar knock at the door, soft and bony. It was him.

You swing the door open; Taterwogen stands in the doorway, lingering just above you. "Today is a day for celebration. Today is the fifth anniversary of your arrival! And to celebrate, I brought a whole pumpkin pie just for you, and a mug of warm apple cider!" He lets go of the mug, and it hovers in the air as if he were still holding it. He reaches over and ruffles your hair, like a proud father. "But most importantly, this is five years free of that nasty habit of yours!"

You smile and take the gifts as you begin to open your mouth; Taterwogen interrupts.

"Don't worry, Young One, I didn't forget!" Taterwogen pulls out a can of whipped topping. "Of course I remember how you like your pie, and remember... You don't have to share with anyone, unless YOU want to"

He smiles happily, the jagged deer skull contorting into a pleasantly warm, friendly smile.

This time, it was your turn to interrupt. "I think... I think I'm ready to go home, Sir."

Taterwogen's smile drops for a second, then returns. "Oh... Alright... Let's get you ready to leave!" Taterwogen hugs you tight.

You reach to hug him back, but you gasp awake in a bedroom that you almost don't recognize; you are back in your childhood home. Looking down, you are sixteen once more; scars and scratches paint your wrists... Tears flood your eyes before... a smell hits you.

Pumpkin Pie.

You look over at your desk; something is dimly lit by a candle.

You clatter out of bed and walk over to find a note, a gift box, and a lit candle.

You lift the note to read it: "I'm so proud of you, Young One, continue being amazing and make sure to visit again soon!"

p.p.s I couldn't send the apple cider; Jeremy gets a little moody if I send liquids... You know how he gets  : )."

You open the box and, sure enough... Your pumpkin pie and topping are still here, and you can't help but smile.

Your gaze returns to the scars on your wrist, and you know that everything will be okay.
